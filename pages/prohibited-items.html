Prohibited & Restricted Items

Customers must not request transport of goods that are unlawful or unsafe to carry.

  • Illegal drugs and controlled substances.
  • Weapons, ammunition and explosives unless lawfully authorised and expressly accepted.
  • Hazardous, flammable or dangerous materials that cannot be lawfully transported under the applicable service.
  • Stolen or unlawfully obtained goods.
  • Any item prohibited by applicable Nigerian law or by the agreed transport service.
If you are unsure whether an item can be transported, contact ML Logistics before booking.
